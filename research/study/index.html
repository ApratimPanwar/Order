Before you start

You will be shown a series of abstract compositions made of simple shapes. For each one you will be asked two questions and given space to explain your answer in your own words.

There are no right or wrong answers. The compositions were produced in several different ways, and we are not told which is which while rating.

  • Nothing identifying is collected — no name, no email, no contact details. Your session is labelled with a random code.
  • Your answers are stored only in this browser until you choose to download them. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • You can stop at any time, and you can erase everything you have entered.
  • This is a development test of the software, not a research study. Responses collected here are for checking that the tool works.
